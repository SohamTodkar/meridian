## A system is a promise you can observe

The most useful systems have a short feedback loop. They tell you what happened, leave room for a human to interpret it, and make the next move obvious.

> Design the smallest artifact that can prove or disprove the next assumption.

### The weekly check

| Signal | Question | Useful response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | Did this feel lighter to repeat? | Remove one decision. |
| Evidence | What changed outside the notebook? | Capture one concrete outcome. |
| Friction | Where did the handoff stall? | Make the handoff visible. |

<Callout title="Working rule">A dashboard is not a system. The behavior it makes easier is the system.</Callout>
