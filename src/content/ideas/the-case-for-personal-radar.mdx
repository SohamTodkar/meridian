## Lists make every signal look equally close

An unordered list hides distance. A radar makes the relationship visible: what is immediate, what is gathering evidence, and what is interesting but not actionable yet.

### The three rings

1. **Now** contains commitments with a clear next action.
2. **Next** contains experiments with a question and a review date.
3. **Later** contains references worth protecting from premature certainty.
