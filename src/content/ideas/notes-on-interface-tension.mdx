## Calm is not the absence of movement

Interfaces can be quiet and still have a pulse. A change in position or opacity is enough when the content hierarchy is already doing the heavy lifting.

### Make the affordance earn its attention

The important motion answers a question: **where did this go, what changed, and what can I do next?** Decorative movement without an answer spends attention without giving anything back.
