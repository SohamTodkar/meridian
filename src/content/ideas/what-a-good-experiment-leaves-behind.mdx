## Evidence is a reusable trace

An experiment is not only a decision. It is also a trace that makes the next decision less expensive. The useful record contains the framing, the observed behavior, and the open question.

### A small evidence format

<CodeBlock language="md" code={`Question: What are we trying to learn?
Signal: What did we observe?
Decision: What changed because of it?
Open edge: What remains uncertain?`} />

<Callout title="Keep it legible">The notes should be understandable by the person who was not in the room.</Callout>
