## Every brief has an interface

A brief is an interface between an intention and a group of people who need to make decisions together. The best ones let people see the trade-offs before the pixels arrive.

### Write the tension down

Do not hide a tension behind a vague objective. Name the competing pressures, then show what will make one direction more credible than the other.

<Callout title="Prompt">What needs to be true for this idea to deserve a second week?</Callout>
