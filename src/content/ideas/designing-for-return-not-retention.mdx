## Return is an invitation, not a trap

Retention language often makes a product grasp for attention. Return-oriented language asks whether the work still has a place in someone’s day.

### The return test

When a person comes back after two weeks, can they answer three questions in under a minute?

- What is this for?
- What changed while I was away?
- What is the smallest useful next step?

<Callout title="A humane metric">A person should be able to leave without losing their place.</Callout>
